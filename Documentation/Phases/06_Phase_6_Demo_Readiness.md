# Phase 6: Demo Readiness & Presentation Strategy (PIE)

> **Document Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Showcase Ready | **Prerequisite:** Phase 5 Complete

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Phase 6 prepares the narrative, executive pitch, and live demonstration to showcase PIE to Engineering Managers, Architects, Data Engineers, and Leadership.

---

## 2. Presentation Storyline
* **Act 1 – The Reality (The 200+ Pipeline Problem):** Fragmented knowledge, no documentation, fear of breaking changes.
* **Act 2 – The Vision:** PIE as the dedicated Engineering Knowledge Layer.
* **Act 3 – The Live Solution:** 8-step interactive demonstration.

---

## 3. The 8-Step Live Demo Script

1. **Connect:** Authenticate with Azure and discover resource groups/factories.
2. **Discover:** Ingest and catalog all pipelines, datasets, linked services, and triggers.
3. **Asset Explorer:** Non-AI visual browsing of pipeline activities and datasets.
4. **Dependency Graph:** Interactive graph visualization showing multi-hop relationships.
5. **AI Explanation:** Ask *“Explain Customer_Load”* -> Technical and Business summaries.
6. **Semantic Discovery:** Ask *“Which pipelines use SAP?”* -> Azure AI Search ranking.
7. **Impact Analysis (Highlight):** Ask *“What happens if I delete CustomerDataset?”* -> Downstream blast radius.
8. **Documentation Export:** One-click export to Git-ready Markdown.

---

## 4. Rehearsal & Backup Fail-Safe Plan
* **Azure Auth failure:** Switch to pre-loaded local cache.
* **AI Foundry latency:** Fall back to deterministic graph view.
* **No internet:** Use synthetic offline demo dataset.

---

## 5. Phase 6 Exit Criteria
* Polished presentation deck, speaker notes, and FAQ preparation.
* Live demonstration rehearsed and verified against fail-safes.
